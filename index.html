FAQ
Frequently asked questions
General
What is Monos?+
Monos is a terminal for Base chain 8453. One screen to trade Base tokens, built by MONOSTECH lab in Jakarta. No multi-chain noise, just Base. Inspired by your M logo — rounded square, thick stroke, mono weight.
How long has Monos been around?+
Monos started in 2025 as internal tool for Monostech. Public launch 2026 on Base. Built for traders who hate 10 tabs.
Is Monos hard to use for beginners?+
No. Connect wallet, pick token, buy/sell one click. Everything in browser, no install. Same UX as umi.bot but for trading not minting.
I heard Monos was unsafe+
Safe by design. Keys stored with 2 layers encryption, private RPC, no MEV. We never store seed. Audit pending.
Usage & Features
Is it safe to use Monos?+
Yes. 2-layer encryption, non-custodial, you keep keys. Same model as umi.bot security.
Can Monos detect which of my wallets are whitelisted?+
Yes. Bulk whitelist checking built-in. Connect multiple wallets, we check all at once.
How fast is Monos? What is the hit rate?+
Sub-100ms with built-in RPC + proxy. No custom RPC needed. Hit rate depends on network, but we batch.
Do failed trades still cost gas?+
Yes, like any Base tx, failed tx still costs gas. But we simulate first to reduce fail.
Do you offer refunds?+
On-demand $2/trade non-refundable. Pro $200/month pausable anytime, see pricing page.
