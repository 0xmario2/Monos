Monos has evolved

As of November 12, 2026, Monos's new terminal has shipped. This includes the Monos web app, multi-chain support, as well as the Monos API.

Monos started nearly one year ago as a simple mint tool I built for myself while exploring Base. It grew into 80,000+ mints and thousands of wallets across all major chains.

To everyone who used Monos, supported us, worked with us, invested in us, advised us, or participated in our community: thank you. Monos would not have existed without you.

Former users can find the new dashboard in Launch App. Check docs for API migration.
