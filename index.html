Monos is evolving

As of November 2026, Monos has shipped its biggest update yet. This includes the new Monos Terminal, multi-chain minting, and the Monos API.

Monos started nearly a year ago as a simple mint tool I built for myself while trying to mint on Base. I never expected it to grow into a product serving more than 80,000 mints and powering thousands of wallets across all major chains.

From the entire team and me, it has been an incredible honour to build Monos for all of you. It was the journey of a lifetime. The highs were very high, the lows were very low, and we learned more than we could have imagined along the way.

We gave Monos everything we had while trying to build the most ambitious onchain minting product possible. There were great bets, bad bets, and plenty in between. While we did not ultimately realize everything in the way we originally hoped, I believe we helped make the onchain economy easier to mint and use for a significant number of people.

To everyone who used Monos, supported us, worked with us, invested in us, advised us, or participated in our community: thank you. Monos would not have existed without you.

Former Monos users can find the new dashboard in Launch App. For API access, check our docs.

For support, contact us through Discord or Twitter.

Let's mint!
