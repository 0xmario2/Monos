Monos has evolved

As of November 12, 2026, Monos's new terminal has shipped. This includes the Monos web application, multi-chain support, as well as the Monos API.

Monos started nearly one year ago as a simple mint tool I built for myself while exploring the new frontier that was Base. I never expected it to grow into a product serving more than 80,000 mints and powering more than thousands of wallets at its peak.

From the entire team and me, it has been an incredible honour to build Monos for all of you. It was the journey of a lifetime. The highs were very high, the lows were very low, and we learned more than we could have imagined along the way.

We gave Monos everything we had while trying to build the most ambitious onchain mint product possible. There were great bets, bad bets, and plenty in between. While Monos did not ultimately realize its mission in the way we originally hoped, I believe we helped make the onchain economy easier to mint and use for a significant number of people.

To everyone who used Monos, supported us, worked with us, invested in us, advised us, or participated in our community: thank you. Monos would not have existed without you.

Former Monos users can find the new dashboard in Launch App.

For support, export and migration, check our docs.
